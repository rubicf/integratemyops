Legal Operations Dashboard

Challenge: The firm has difficulty seeing which administrative tasks are outstanding and who is responsible for follow-up.

Intake workflow map

Client Intake & Onboarding Workflow

Challenge: New client inquiries move inconsistently from first contact to engagement and handoff.

Solution: A defined path covering intake forms, conflict checks, engagement letters, and team handoff with clear ownership at each step.

Intended benefit: Consistent, trackable intake and fewer dropped handoffs.

SOP examples

Standard Operating Procedures

Challenge: Recurring administrative activities live in people’s heads and vary by staff member.

Solution: Written procedures for document requests, task assignment, and client communication so the firm isn’t dependent on any one person.

Intended benefit: Consistency, easier onboarding, and clearer expectations.

Operational Assessment & Improvement Roadmap

Challenge: Leadership knows operations feel messy but lacks a structured view of priorities and sequencing.

Solution: A full assessment-style report organizing findings, priority ratings, recommendations, and a phased 90-day plan.

Intended benefit: A concrete plan management can use to drive improvements.
