Services

Practical support for law firm operations

We start with a focused assessment. Follow-on services help you implement improvements and keep operations running smoothly as the firm grows.

Primary launch service

Legal Operations Assessment

Identify operational inefficiencies and receive a practical improvement plan. Ideal for firms that want clarity before investing in additional staff, software, or ongoing support.

What it includes

  • 1Initial discovery meeting
  • 2Review of up to three agreed workflows
  • 3Review of task tracking and administrative processes
  • 4Review of software utilization
  • 5Assessment of client management and administrative procedures
  • 6Written findings and recommendations
  • 730-, 60-, and 90-day improvement roadmap
  • 8Presentation of findings

Assessment categories

Client management

Intake, onboarding, communication, and follow-ups

Case administration

Administrative status tracking, document requests, outstanding items

Workflow management

Task assignments, responsibilities, internal procedures

Systems & technology

Clio or other platforms, spreadsheets, CRM utilization

Reporting

Outstanding tasks, workload visibility, management reports

Additional services

Available as follow-on engagements after an assessment—or when you already know the area you want to improve.

Workflow & Process Development

Turn operational recommendations into repeatable processes. Typical projects include client intake workflows, SOPs, task assignment structures, and staff onboarding checklists.

Systems & Reporting Development

Improve operational visibility and make better use of existing technology: Clio configuration planning, task tracking, management dashboards, and reporting templates.

Fractional Legal Operations Support

Ongoing operational oversight without a full-time operations hire. Monitoring KPIs, reviewing workflow effectiveness, coordinating process improvements, and supporting management reporting as the firm grows.

Pricing for workflow, systems, and fractional work is scoped per engagement. Contact us to discuss scope and timeline.
