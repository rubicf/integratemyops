Our process

From discovery to a clear plan

A structured, collaborative review designed to give firm leaders a practical picture of how operations work today—and a prioritized path forward.

Discover

We learn about your firm, practice areas, team, goals, and the operational challenges you already see.

Assess

We walk through agreed workflows, tools, and administrative processes with the people who use them every day.

Recommend

We share findings: bottlenecks, duplicated effort, unclear ownership, and what’s already working. Recommendations are prioritized by impact and effort.

Plan

You receive a written report and a 30-, 60-, and 90-day roadmap, plus a presentation of findings.

Your assessment deliverables

Eight components designed to give you both clarity and a usable action plan.

01

Executive Summary

A concise overview of operational condition, the most significant findings, and key opportunities.

02

Current Operational Structure

A documented snapshot of how administrative operations, responsibilities, workflows, and reporting work today.

03

Findings by Category

Structured review across agreed areas: client management, case administration, workflows, systems, and reporting.

04

Identified Inefficiencies

Process gaps, duplicated work, inconsistent procedures, bottlenecks, and unclear responsibilities.

05

Risk & Priority Ratings

Findings ranked by operational impact, urgency, and effort required so you know what to address first.

06

Recommended Improvements

Practical recommendations tied to the findings—workflows, accountability, procedures, and visibility.

07

Suggested Systems & Tools

Recommendations for software, trackers, dashboards, or templates matched to your firm’s needs.

08

30 / 60 / 90-Day Roadmap

A phased plan with suggested sequencing, responsibilities, and milestones for the next three months.
